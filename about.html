Our Story

From a Spreadsheet Headache to an Enterprise Platform

ApexHR was founded by a small team of HR operations and engineering professionals who were tired of watching leave approvals get lost in email threads and shared spreadsheets. We believed workforce tools could be as intuitive as the consumer apps people use every day.

What began as an internal tool for a single fast-growing startup quickly turned into a full enterprise portal, now trusted by HR desks and employees across dozens of organizations to track balances, automate approvals, and keep teams running smoothly.

Our focus has never changed: build a fast, secure, beautifully designed leave management experience for both the people requesting time off and the admins who keep everything organized.

Our Mission & Values

Every feature we ship is guided by a few core principles that shape how we build ApexHR.

🎯

Clarity First

Every balance, request, and approval status should be understandable at a glance — no confusing spreadsheets or buried emails.

🤝

Built for Both Sides

We design equally for the employee submitting a request and the admin reviewing it, so the whole workflow feels effortless.

🔐

Security & Trust

Role-based access and careful session handling keep sensitive workforce data protected at every step.

⚡

Speed & Reliability

Real-time balance sync and instant notifications mean nobody is left wondering about the status of their request.

🌱

Always Improving

We ship iteratively based on real HR team feedback, refining workflows instead of overloading the product with clutter.

💬

Human Support
