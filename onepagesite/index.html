Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about ApexHR Portal.

How do I test the application as an Employee or Admin?
Simply click on the "Sign In" or "Launch Portal" buttons to go to the login page. On the login page, you can use the "⚡ Fast Demo Logins" buttons to instantly log in as an Employee (Alex Johnson) or Admin (System Administrator).
Does the portal calculate leave duration automatically?
Yes! When applying for leave, selecting a start date and end date automatically computes the exact number of days requested and validates against available leave balances.
Is my data saved across browser refreshes?
Yes, all employee profiles, leave application records, and approval status updates are stored safely in browser LocalStorage.
Can I return to the Home page from the Login or Dashboard views?
Yes! The login screen and all dashboard views include prominent "Back to Home" links in their navigation headers.
